ADD-ON NAME
========================================

AI Chat Exporter and Printer

========================================
SUMMARY (250 characters max)
========================================

Export and print ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini conversations to PDF, Markdown, HTML, DOCX, JSON, and TXT. Preserve code artifacts, web citations, images, and formatting. 100% private - all processing happens locally in your browser.

========================================
DESCRIPTION
========================================

Export your AI conversations with professional formatting and complete privacy. AI Chat Exporter makes it easy to save and share your conversations with ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini in multiple formats.

✨ What's New in v1.1.1

Right-Click Context Menu:

• Quick Export Access - Right-click anywhere on the page to export directly to multiple formats
• Quick Print Access - Right-click to print conversations in any format with a single click
• Hierarchical Menus - Organized Export and Print menus with format submenus for easy access
• Faster Workflow - No need to open the extension popup for common export and print operations
• Instant Action - Export or print with just two clicks from the context menu

Print Support:

📋 How to Use

Method 1: Extension Popup
1. Navigate to ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini
2. Click the AI Chat Exporter icon in your toolbar
3. Choose your preferred export format
4. Select which messages to export (or export all)
5. Click "Export" and your formatted conversation downloads immediately

Method 2: Right-Click Context Menu ⚡ NEW!
1. Navigate to ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini
2. Right-click anywhere on the page
3. Hover over "Export" or "Print" in the context menu
4. Select your desired format from the submenu
5. Your conversation is instantly exported or sent to the printer

Method 3: Keyboard Shortcut
• Press Ctrl+Shift+E (Windows/Linux) or Cmd+Shift+E (Mac) to open the export dialog

Method 4: In-Page Buttons
• Use the export buttons integrated directly into the chat interface
