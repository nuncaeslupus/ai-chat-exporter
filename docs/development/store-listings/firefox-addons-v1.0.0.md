# Firefox Add-ons Listing - AI Chat Exporter and Printer

## Add-on Name
AI Chat Exporter and Printer

## Summary (250 characters max)
Export and print ChatGPT conversations to PDF, Markdown, DOCX, HTML, TXT, and JSON. Save and print your AI chats easily. No data collection - everything stays local in your browser.

## Description

Save and print your ChatGPT conversations in multiple formats with AI Chat Exporter and Printer.

**Features:**
- Export to PDF, Markdown, DOCX, HTML, TXT, and JSON
- Print conversations directly from ChatGPT
- Preserves code blocks and formatting
- Custom filename templates
- No account required
- No data collection - everything stays local

**Supported Platforms:**
- ChatGPT (chat.openai.com, chatgpt.com)
- More coming...

**How to Use:**
1. Open a ChatGPT conversation
2. Click the extension icon in your browser toolbar
3. Choose your preferred export format
4. Click Export or Print

**Privacy:**
This extension runs entirely in your browser. No conversation data is sent to external servers or collected by the extension.

## Privacy Policy

AI Chat Exporter does not collect, store, or transmit any user data.

**What the extension does:**
- Reads conversation content from ChatGPT pages when you click Export
- Saves your preferences (like last selected format) locally in your browser

**What the extension does NOT do:**
- Does not send data to external servers
- Does not track your usage
- Does not collect personal information
- Does not share data with third parties

All processing happens locally in your browser. Exported files are saved directly to your computer.

## Permissions

**Access your data for chat.openai.com and chatgpt.com:**
Required to read conversation content from ChatGPT pages so it can be exported to your chosen format.

**Store unlimited amount of client-side data:**
Used to save user preferences (like last selected export format) locally in your browser.
