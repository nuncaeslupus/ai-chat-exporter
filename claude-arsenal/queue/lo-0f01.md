## Work

`eslint.config.js` enables `strictTypeChecked` + `stylisticTypeChecked` and had never actually run (its two imports were missing from `package.json` until PR #4). With lint working, the codebase reports **1151 errors / 97 warnings**.

Decide first, then execute:

1. **Right-size the config** — `strictTypeChecked` is a deliberate choice nobody has ever lived with here. `recommendedTypeChecked` plus a few opt-ins may be the honest baseline, and turns most of the 1151 into zero without touching source.
2. **Fix what remains** — `pnpm lint:fix` clears 67 automatically; the rest are mostly `no-confusing-void-expression`, `restrict-template-expressions` and `no-non-null-assertion` in tests and helpers.

Whichever path, the endpoint is the same: lint green, lint in CI.
