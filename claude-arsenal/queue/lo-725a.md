# Payload: lo-725a — Collapse rendered maths to one representation

**Gate**: the inline and display formulas in
`tests/fixtures/dom-snapshots/chatgpt/formatting-showcase-2026-07.html` each appear
**exactly once** in every export format; the Gemini KaTeX case in
`tests/unit/core/parsers/base-parser.test.ts` still passes.

## The defect

`lo-74ed` (merged, PR #11) stopped `cleanupElement()` stripping `aria-hidden` inside
`.katex`, because on Gemini `.katex-html` is the *only* copy of the formula and
stripping it lost the maths entirely.

Live survey of ChatGPT 2026-07-28 shows the opposite shape:

| part | ChatGPT | Gemini |
|---|---|---|
| `.katex-mathml` | present | **absent** |
| `annotation[encoding="application/x-tex"]` (LaTeX source) | present | absent |
| `.katex-html` (`aria-hidden="true"`) | present | present |

Measured `textContent` of a single ChatGPT `.katex`:

```
"E=mc2E = mc^2E=mc2"
```

— the MathML rendering, the LaTeX source, and the visual glyphs, concatenated. So
the formula is **triplicated** in every ChatGPT export today.

Neither "strip aria-hidden" nor "keep aria-hidden" is correct for both platforms.

## Work

Treat `.katex` / `mjx-container` as an **atomic unit** and collapse it to one
representation, in preference order:

1. `annotation[encoding="application/x-tex"]` — the real LaTeX source, the only
   lossless option
2. `.katex-mathml` text
3. `.katex-html` text

Then no generic aria-hidden rule needs to know about maths at all. Consider whether
the `lo-74ed` carve-out in `cleanupElement()` should be *replaced* by this rather
than layered on top — say which you chose.

Emitting real LaTeX also gives the exporters something better than glyph soup;
check whether any exporter can render it (and do not add a maths dependency to find
out — out of scope).

Read `docs/dev/parser-gotchas.md` §3.
