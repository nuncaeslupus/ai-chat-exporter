## Decision, 2026-07-29: hold until the exporters redesign lands

The owner chose to **wait for the exporters redesign** rather than ship 1.2.0 now.
Reasoning, recorded so it is not re-litigated:

- The redesign changes how **every** export looks. Shipping now would submit a
  build whose output is superseded within days, then need a second submission.
- **Two store re-reviews are already pending**: `scripting` (PR #116) and
  `*.web-sandbox.oaiusercontent.com` (PR #153). A third submission stacked on a
  pending queue is where reviews get slow and confusing.

**Dependencies are all met** — `lo-485e`, `lo-1316`, `lo-8d36`, `lo-f096`,
`lo-adf1`, `lo-ad6c` are all merged. Nothing technical blocks this task; it is
held on the redesign by choice. Do **not** treat it as ready just because
`queue_batch.sh` offers it.
