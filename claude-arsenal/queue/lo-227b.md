# Payload: lo-227b — PDF sanitizer deletes characters it could render

**Gate**: `~`, `°` and `·` survive a PDF export.

`src/core/utils/pdf-characters.ts:52` — the sanitizer drops characters that **are** representable in Win-1252 (tilde, degree sign, middle dot among them) rather than substituting or passing them through. Silent deletion, so the export looks fine until someone reads it closely — degree signs disappearing from a technical conversation is the kind of corruption nobody catches until it matters.

Fix: pass through everything Win-1252 can encode; substitute only what it genuinely cannot, and use a visible replacement rather than deletion so loss is detectable.

Test: round-trip a string of the affected characters through the sanitizer and assert each is present or explicitly substituted — never silently removed.
