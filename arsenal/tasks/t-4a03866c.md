### Finding
addExtension strips everything after the last '.' of the stem as if it were an old extension, but buildFilename passes it a stem built from the title. A chat titled 'Fix main.py error' with the default {title}_{date} template is saved as 'Fix-main.pdf' - the rest of the title and the date are lost, and distinct chats collide on one name.

### Evidence
traced code path (node_modules absent, tests could not be run): sanitizeFilename (l.144) keeps '.', stem 'Fix-main.py-error_2026-11-11' matches /\.[^.]*$/ from '.py-error_...'. tests/unit/core/services/filename-service.test.ts:349-354 only pin the 'replace a real extension' case.

### Suggested direction
Do not strip in buildFilename (the stem never carries an extension); either drop the strip or replace dots in the title during sanitisation.
