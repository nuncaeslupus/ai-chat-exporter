```bash
pnpm vitest run tests/unit/extension/content/scroll-to-load.test.ts && pnpm validate
```

## Problem

chatgpt.com and claude.ai both **virtualize** conversation turns — nodes are
mounted and unmounted as you scroll, not merely hidden with
`content-visibility` — so a parse only ever sees the rendered window and the
export silently loses everything outside it.

Measured live on 2026-11-13:

| page | state | message units in DOM | true total |
|---|---|---|---|
| chatgpt.com, 3 exchanges | at rest after load | 4 | 6 |
| same page | scrolled to top | 2 | 6 |
| claude.ai | at rest vs. scrolled to top | user/assistant 2/3 | 3/4 |

Two consequences:

1. **Silent truncation.** The ChatGPT conversation above exports 2 of its 3
   exchanges, oldest dropped, with no warning — the worst kind of failure for
   an archival tool.
2. **False "no conversation".** When the rendered window holds no complete
   user+assistant pair, `extractQAPairs` returns 0 and the popup shows its
   `noConversation` state even though every selector is healthy. This is
   indistinguishable, to the user, from the selector drift fixed in #292.

## Shape of the fix

- `parseConversation()` in `src/extension/content/content-script.ts` is
  **synchronous** and is the single chokepoint for all three parse paths
  (`get_conversation`, export, print). A scroll-to-load pass has to await
  re-renders, so it becomes `async` and the three call sites follow.
- `custom.scrollContainer` already exists in
  `src/core/parsers/claude/selectors.ts` and **is read by nothing in `src/`** —
  wire it up rather than inventing a second mechanism.
- ChatGPT needs a `scrollContainer` of its own: the live scroller is an inner
  `main` descendant, not `document.scrollingElement`. Find it the way the probe
  did — the first `main div` whose `scrollHeight` exceeds its `clientHeight`.
- Bound the loop (turn count stops growing, or a hard cap), **restore the
  user's scroll position** afterwards, and do not fight a streaming turn
  (`data-is-streaming="true"` on Claude; skip the pass while one is live).

## Tests to write failing first

- `tests/unit/extension/content/scroll-to-load.test.ts`
  - collects turns that were never simultaneously mounted: a jsdom container
    that unmounts turns outside a window and mounts others on `scrollTop`
    change, asserting the parse sees **all** of them.
  - restores the original `scrollTop` when the pass finishes.
  - terminates when the turn count stops growing, and when a cap is hit.
  - leaves a streaming conversation alone.
