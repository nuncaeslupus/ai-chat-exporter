## Acceptance gate

**Gate**: a conversation title of CJK or emoji characters produces a filename that saves successfully on all three OSes.


Prose-only gate — verified by worker judgment, no script to run.

`src/core/services/filename-service.ts:76` truncates by UTF-16 code-unit count. Filesystem limits are in **bytes** (255 on ext4/APFS/NTFS). A 200-"character" CJK title is ~600 UTF-8 bytes and the save fails or gets silently mangled; emoji are surrogate pairs, so slicing can also cut one in half and emit an invalid sequence.

Fix: measure with `TextEncoder`, truncate on a code-point boundary, and test with CJK, emoji and combining marks.
