## Work

1. **Conversation block** — title 17/700, and a meta line of
   `<platform icon> Platform · N pairs · date range` with `tabular-nums`. The
   **date range comes from the conversation's own message timestamps**, not from
   today; if timestamps are missing, drop that segment rather than inventing one.
   No word count here (it lives in the R4 footer).
2. **Setting rows** (38 px, `margin-top:auto`): `Contenido` with its value on the
   right (`Toda la conversación` / `3 de 14 pares`) and `Opciones` with **no**
   counter. Both are navigation rows into R4/R5 — wire them to the R1 router;
   the views themselves are not yours.
3. **Action bar**: split button 50 px — left half exports the current format,
   right half (42 px, divider, chevron) toggles `formatMenuOpen` for R3. Print
   button 50×50, disabled for DOCX — **keep the existing `printableFormats`
   logic**, do not restate the list. Privacy line, always visible.
4. Export/print keep today's message contracts (`export_conversation`,
   `print_conversation`, `format`, `selectedIndices`) and today's
   warning handling: on `response.warning` the popup stays open and switches to
   the warning state (R7 renders it — emit the state, do not draw the card).

New i18n keys go into **all seven** `_locales/*/messages.json` in this PR.
