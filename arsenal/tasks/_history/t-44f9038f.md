### Finding
Preferences (including user-typed filename literal pieces), theme and language are written to chrome.storage.sync, which uploads them to the user's browser account and other devices. PRIVACY.md says chrome.storage 'stays on your device' and the store listing says preferences are saved 'locally in your browser' - a claim a store reviewer can falsify.

### Evidence
Read storage.ts: only last_export_format uses storage.local (l.158,173); grep of the two docs for 'storage'.

### Suggested direction
Switch to chrome.storage.local (with a one-time migration) or correct both documents to say settings sync through the browser account.
